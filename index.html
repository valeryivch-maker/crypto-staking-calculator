How Crypto Staking Rewards Work

Crypto staking allows holders of Proof-of-Stake (PoS) cryptocurrencies like Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), and Polkadot (DOT) to earn passive income by helping secure the blockchain network. Instead of sitting idle in a wallet, your tokens generate yield paid out in additional coins.

Why Compounding Frequency Matters

When you reinvest your staking rewards back into the pool (compounding), you begin earning interest on your interest. Daily or monthly compounding significantly boosts your effective Annual Percentage Yield (APY) over long time horizons compared to simple interest.
